## Data Source & Market Attribution

HAWKLYX monitors Brent crude oil benchmark pricing (`BRENT_CRUDE_USD`).
- **Data Provider**: Upstream commodity pricing data is fetched via [OilPriceAPI](https://www.oilpriceapi.com/).
- **Usage Scope**: The data provider's support team confirmed that multi-account allowance pooling is acceptable within this personal, educational, testing, and non-commercial project scope.
- **Disclaimer**: HAWKLYX provides market data for personal, educational, and observational purposes only. It is neither intended for nor certified as real-time financial trading, broker execution, or investment advisory. Market quotes may be subject to upstream exchange reporting delays and caching intervals.
